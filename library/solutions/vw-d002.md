# Solution: Choose a useful first reliability objective

`VW-D002` · Solution and reasoning

## Suggested reasoning

Thirty days × 100000 gives 3000000 eligible requests. A 99.9% success objective permits 3000 bad requests in that simplified window. This is a teaching calculation, not a recommended business commitment. Request success is closer to user experience than host uptime, but application-only logs miss upstream failures. Add external measurement with clearly documented sampling limitations.

## Decision record

Define success (status and response semantics), eligible endpoints, retry treatment and missing-data policy. Gather customer-impact and baseline data before agreeing on the target. Use a provisional review date and explicit coverage hours. When budget is exhausted, reduce discretionary change, inspect burn causes and prioritise reliability; emergency fixes need a deliberate exception route. Separate critical checkout-like flows from informational endpoints when their expectations differ.
