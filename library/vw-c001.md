# A green deployment and a failed user request

`VW-C001` · beginner · 20 minutes · draft · free/local

## Question

Simulation: release R42 passes its pipeline and all processes are running. Checkout requests return 503 for 18% of users. A teammate says, “Delivery succeeded, so operations owns this.” What does a useful definition of success include, and who should investigate?

## Task

Write three separate statements: what the pipeline proves, what the user evidence proves, and what remains unknown. Propose one shared outcome metric and one feedback action. Explain why adding a manual approval alone may not improve reliability.

## Verification

Your answer must distinguish process health from request success, assign a joint investigation without blame, and specify a measurable denominator and time window.

## Reflection

When could a fully running service still be unavailable? What evidence would justify stopping the rollout?
