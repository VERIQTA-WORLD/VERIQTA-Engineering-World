# Solution: A passing pipeline publishes a secret

`VW-I002` · Solution and reasoning

## Evidence and conclusion

Shell tracing exposed a header. Success of the HTTP command says nothing about confidentiality. In a real event, follow the credential owner’s revocation/rotation process, restrict access and preserve required audit evidence; investigate downstream use before considering the incident closed.

## Plausible alternatives

A test sentinel could be public by design, but real tokens must be treated as exposed until scope is established. Logs alone do not show misuse. Check credential audit records without publishing them.

## Recovery verification

Disable tracing around sensitive operations, remove unnecessary credential access, rotate real credentials and verify the previous credential is invalid. Re-run with a fake sentinel; confirm no sentinel in logs, artifacts or summaries.

## Prevention and trade-offs

Prefer scoped short-lived credentials and minimise log retention. Redaction is defence in depth, not permission to print secrets. Auditability and privacy require an agreed evidence-retention procedure.
