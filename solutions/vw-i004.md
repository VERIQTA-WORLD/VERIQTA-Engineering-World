# Solution: The error alert that goes quiet during failure

`VW-I004` · Author guidance; independently unreviewed.

## Evidence and conclusion

The ratio cannot establish health once its inputs disappear. The external probe supplies independent failure evidence. The ten-minute pending period also prevents shorter observed threshold crossings from firing. Missing telemetry should not silently reset the only availability signal.

## Plausible alternatives

Low request volume, scrape relabelling errors and exporter outages can resemble service outages. Inspect scrape health, series presence and multiple probe locations. A single probe failure may be a probe-path fault.

## Recovery verification

Test normal traffic, sustained errors, zero traffic and missing series separately using promtool rule tests when available. Verify that missing telemetry produces an actionable signal and a real recovery resolves it. Offline reasoning alone does not validate PromQL execution.

## Prevention and trade-offs

Use independent probes and telemetry health alerts. Avoid turning every absent metric into a page; choose service ownership labels and severity intentionally.
