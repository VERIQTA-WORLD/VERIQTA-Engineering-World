# Choose a useful first reliability objective

`VW-D002` · advanced · 45 minutes · draft · free/local

## Scenario

Simulation: a public status API handles 100000 eligible requests daily. Product proposes 100% monthly availability. Operations has one engineer on call and cannot safely provide immediate response all day. Clients retry, but some requests fail before reaching the app. Logs contain application status only.

## Constraints

Choose a measurable objective and a feasible response policy. Budget is fixed. Do not invent missing customer-impact data. Use a thirty-day teaching window with uniform daily traffic only for arithmetic.

## Task

Compare a request success SLI, an external probe and infrastructure uptime. Propose a provisional SLO, exclusions, missing-data policy and review date. Calculate an illustrative 99.9% request error budget. Discuss how deployment policy might change when it is exhausted.

## Verification

Show the denominator and arithmetic. Explain why the illustrative budget does not prove an appropriate target or staffing model. Provide an alternative for critical and noncritical endpoints.

## Trade-offs

Contrast user coverage, probe-path bias and operational load. Record missing evidence before committing to an objective.

## Reflection

How could an exclusion hide the most important failures?
